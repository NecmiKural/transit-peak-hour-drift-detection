When does rush hour really start?

Historical estimates, replayed as a stream. Three months (March to May 2024) of estimated 5-minute boarding counts for 10 Salvador bus stops, from the SUNT dataset (CC BY 4.0).

Six-person group project for COMPX523 at the University of Waikato. Necmi Kural's part was Role D: the adaptive boundary detector, the ADWIN and Page-Hinkley drift tests and the schedule alignment. Full credit for every role is in the README.

Does anything drift? Not at the right granularity

CapyMOA ADWIN on the online regressor's error, per stop. Fed every 5-minute error, it alarms dozens of times because it tracks the daily demand cycle. Fed the per-day RMSE, it finds no drift day at any stop, which matches the boundary series.

From outputs/role_d_concept_drift.csv, the CapyMOA run.
