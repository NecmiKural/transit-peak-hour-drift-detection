# Transit peak hours and drift

Charts from [transit-peak-hour-drift-detection](https://github.com/NecmiKural/transit-peak-hour-drift-detection), a six-person COMPX523 group project at the University of Waikato: online detection of when each Salvador bus stop's morning and evening peaks really start, and ADWIN tests for whether they drift over March to May 2024. Necmi Kural's part was Role D (change-point and drift); full credit for every role is in the repository README.

- **Historical estimates, replayed as a stream.** The boarding counts are estimates from the [SUNT](https://github.com/LabIA-UFBA/SUNT) dataset (CC BY 4.0), not redistributed here; the page shows only results derived from them.
- The page is plain HTML with inline SVG, generated by `space/build.py` from the CSVs in `outputs/`. It has no scripts and loads nothing from other sites.
